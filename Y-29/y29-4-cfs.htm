Thanks to the efforts of the Fightertown Design Group you can now download and fly the virtual aircraft of the Legends of Y-29.  This 5 plane set for Microsoft Combat Flight Simulator 2 includes the aircraft of J.C. Meyer, commander of the 487th fighter squadron, William Halton, William Whisner and Alden Rigby as well as a generic blue nosed Mustang of the 487th fighter squadron.  Each aircraft is a detail reproduction of the original and includes CFS2 style animation and damage effects.

And best of all they're FREE!
