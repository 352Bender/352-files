Published 11-15-2010



Useful sites and the homes of friendly squadrons

Flight Sim sites and forums of interest
CFS IP Games is a wonderful community of flight sim enthusiasts whose good nature and willingness to help those seeking sound advice is always a pleasure to visit.
   
Netwings is a community of dedicated flight sim enthusiasts that maintains a fine file download section and a well attended forum. Many fine contributors to the forums will provide assistance to those in need.
   
ComCentral is another community of dedicated flight sim and combat flight sim enthusiasts with an active forum set and helpful participants.
   
SimHQ is a community devoted to simulations of all kinds, including combat flight sims. A great deal of very detailed help is available in their forums.
   
Useful Sites
HyperLobby is a user-friendly and efficient gaming area. Similar to the Zone in CFS, HyperLobby acts as a meeting place and staging area for all kinds of flight sims and has a strong following and community. The software is free, easy to use, and available at the link on the left.
   
TeamSpeak is a terrific adjunct to Forgotten Battles, allowing voice communication over multiple channels on a single IP with high and adjustable quality and minimal bandwidth cost. A lot more useful than Roger Wilco, but not as easy to initially set up. Like Roger Wilco, TeamSpeak is free and can be obtained by clicking on the link on the left.
   
   

Friendly Squadrons
(In no particular order)
The AKA Wardogs is a related group of pilots flying everything well. This group is filled with fine pilots and excellent competitors. The 352nd Virtual Fighter Group first met and joined with the AKA when flying in the AKAFA campaigns, found a group of kindred spirits, and salutes this fine group of fellow combat flight sim pilots.
   
Fallen Angels is an incredibily talented group of pilots hosting on-line servers that are incomparable. This group is filled with fine pilots and excellent competitors.
   
Among the most enjoyable pilots with which to fly the virtual skies, the AAA squad represents the best traditions of virtual combat (the 352ndFG excepted, of course, LOL)
   
The K9 Squadron is unique; its humorous but serious approach to the world of combat simulations is incomparable.
   
The 353rd FG Squadron is, like the others listed here, comprised of pilots that enjoy the combat simming world and fly and engage with vigor, fairness, and skill. They also are fun, fair, skilled, organized, and game for virtual combat as gentlemen and competitors. Their website is currently undergoing a revamping, and can be found here.
