Bushpilot

Published 08-05-2014

Successful Bomb Run
Hometown: 6 meters below sealevel
Age: 35
Web Page, Projects, Special Interests, etc: Everything which involves flying
Profile: Anything involving flying makes my heart beat faster. The roar of a SBD-5 engine, or the overpass of a F-16, or the whopping sound of Chinook, it still makes me run to the window to have a look. As a hobby I build my own RC airplanes and at the moment I am building a SBD-5, 1/5.5th scale. The nice thing about airplanes is that it's mechanics only, a big gas engine, a lot of stringers and balsa wood, a load of 10 kg servos and it still doesn't talk back, which is fine by me...He I enjoy working with my hands instead of my head.

I joined the RNSF (KCT) in 1998, went as a JCO (JOint Commission Observer) to Bosnia-Hercegovina in 2000-2001 with my unit. After my tour I started teaching at a secondary school. At the moment I manage an English Dept at that same Secondary School and I teach the subjects English and Physical Education, teaching grade 8-11.

One of my dreams is to get my PPL license someday, but for the time being I have found my niche: the 352nd. Flying in FSX is great, and a trip over the Tongass Fjords is amazing as landing on a small dirt strip with an overloaded bushplane as well, it is as realistic as a modern sim has to offer nowadays, but it still misses something for me.

And so I wake up by the sound of my phone, sneak out of the master bedroom, carefull not to wake up the kids, and start up the sim..., say good evening to fellow flight enthusiasts and let the simming begin!

Flying with a squadron is something to be experienced and cannot be caught in words. Flying historical campaigns with real flight data from WWII , what can beat that?

nunc aut nunquam!
