A Day at the Races

Published 12-31-2010



A brief photo essay....

Created November 7, 2004



In early May, 24 352nd Ramblin' Jack sponsored and ran a race event, creating a race map at Dzankoy. While not all of the 352nd pilots were able to participate in the event, those that did put on a great show. The crowd went wild at the daring displayed by the likes of Oscar, flying the "Dago Red", Hoss (who cut Oscar no slack at all), Crater in his brand new, shiny polished aluminum P-51, Link in his beautiful "A WD", Clay Pigeon in his stock 352nd "HO-C", and of course, everyone's favorite, Herr Brigand in his "Captured P-51".

The races were run in qualifying laps, two heats, and a main event. Herr Brigand put on quite a show in the first heat, closely tailing Oscar's "Dago Red" until receiving deceptive information that the next-to-last lap was the "final lap", hence quitting early. When he learned the truth, Brigand put the pedal to the medal and closed to within mere inches of Dago Red, but lost out to Oscar's fine piloting. Clay Pigeon had slipped several hundred Deutschmarks to Brigand's pit crew, however, and the pin they had loosened in his landing gear finally fell out as Brigand landed, causing his plane to land on only one gear, hence being unflyable and our glorious hero and crowd favorite was unable to participate in the evening's main event. Many of the crowd voiced their displeasure at this deceptive turn of events, but Brigand, ever the gentleman, tipped his hat to the remaining competitors and eagerly watched the main event unfold.

A movie of the main event is available from our "Movies" page.
