Captured German P-51-D

This plane was also required for 352nd Members.

This one needs an explanation (don't ya think??).

During WWII Germany occasionally captured allied aircraft and repainted them in German colors. These aircraft were used for evaluation and, on some occasions, in combat. At the 352nd we love our Mustangs so we have taken advantage of this little known fact to create an Axis plane that allows us to fly our beloved Mustangs when we are required to fly the axis side.

Features: Same as the P-51D Night Fighter.

B-24 "Ball of Fire" of the 93rd Bomb Group

This is the stock B-24 painted as the "Ball of Fire" attached to the 93rd bomb group stationed in Bodney, England. This paint scheme is an excellent example of an Assembly Ship. These aircraft were older and not deemed combat worthy. They were painted in high visibility colors so that they could be easily identified. These ships were used to facilitate assembly by groups for large raids. Once the formation was assembled these ships left formation and headed for base. They were usually manned by crews that had completed their tour and were waiting to be shipped home or were recovering from injuries. Often these ships carried photographers, journalists and VIP's.

The B-24 had a reputation for being hard to start and hard to fly. This virtual version is no exception. You will need to set throttle to 25% to get it started. I suggest using a long runway and reducing fuel to 50% or less to get off of the ground.

No modifications have been made to the air and dp file except to make the AI ship player flyable.

by Bill "Razor" Ross
