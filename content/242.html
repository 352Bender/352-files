Savannah Reunion - Lobby

Published 01-22-2013



Opening Activities



To start the Gathering off, members of the 352nd Fighter Group Association, lead by Bob "Punchy" Powell and his lovely wife Betty, set up and manned a memorabilia table. Veteran members of the Association, their families, friends, and associates all gathered, met, renewed old friendships, visited, and proudly shared memories and friendships. A handsome P-51d windshield exhibit was set up, Troy White, artiste extraordinaire showed his latest painting of "Sweet and Lovely" as well as many of his earlier prints, veterans autographed books, fly jackets, and anything else fans presented, and generally folks simply enjoyed the comraderie generated by the gathering.

We've gathered together some representative images from those hours in the "Lobby". Click on each image to view a larger, 1024x768, image.
