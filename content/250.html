Savannah Reunion - Don Bryan

Published 01-22-2013



October, 2004 Don Bryan, Ace


Flying virtually, for his first time, and doing very well indeed



Bob "Punchy" Powell introduces Don Bryan as the 352nd Fighter Groups highest scoring surviving ace. As well as being an Ace he is one truly fine gentleman and a heck of a sport.

Our own Mr. and Mrs. Gator arrived from their home somewhere in the deep, dark swamps of central Florida to attend the reunion and brought with them a copy of our flight sim, IL2 Sturmovik Forgotten Battles with the Ace Expansion Pack (now, isn't that a mouthful?) loaded on a laptop computer complete with a trusty Microsoft Force Feedback joystick. Gator's object was to demonstrate to those in attendance what the "virtuals" do in their spare time. Our own CO Deacon, never one to ignore an opportunity, offered an opportunity to try out the sim to Don Bryan who initially declined, answering something to the effect of "what do you think I am, crazy??"

Deacon is nothing if not persistent, and the ever-sporting Don Bryan ultimately relented and gave his best efforts. In fact he gave flying lessons to Gator who is still reeling (as Gator's sometimes do) at what he witnessed.

Here, for your enjoyment, are some images of those fateful moments. Note the concentration, then grins, on Don's face as he tries to figure out how to fly a computer that thinks it's a Mustang.
